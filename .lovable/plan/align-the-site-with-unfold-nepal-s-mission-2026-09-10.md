# Align the site with Unfold Nepal's mission

## Content direction
- Reframe the site around helping foreign returnees and the Nepali diaspora turn overseas skills, ideas, networks, and experience into sustainable businesses in Nepal.
- Emphasize entrepreneurship training, awareness, mentorship, practical guidance, strategic support, and meaningful employment.
- Use the line: “We believe every Nepali abroad carries a piece of Nepal's future. Our work is to help them unfold it.”
- Rewrite affected homepage, About, Diaspora, research, book, shared footer, metadata, and supporting copy in a natural human voice.
- Remove em dashes and en dashes from all user-facing copy across the site, replacing them with commas, periods, colons, or separate sentences.

## About page
- Replace the research-house framing with the new company purpose.
- Keep a concise About section, a Mission section only, legal status, focused company objectives, Governance, and the Board of Directors cards.
- Remove History, Vision, current priorities, board enquiry details, the board-page button, and the support and volunteer buttons.
- Remove the dedicated Board of Directors page and every navigation link to it.

## Research page
- Replace the current header with a full-width photographic banner that reaches both edges.
- Remove sector and province filters, report counts, and empty-filter messaging.
- Display only three reports in a clean fixed grid.
- Adjust the introduction so research supports returnees' real business decisions and the wider entrepreneurial ecosystem.

## 100 Businesses page
- Replace “flagship” wording with a more grounded label such as “Core publication.”
- Shorten the cover caption to “Cover design in development.”
- Limit coverage to six districts near Kathmandu: Kathmandu, Lalitpur, Bhaktapur, Kavrepalanchok, Nuwakot, and Dhading.
- Update coverage wording to match six districts.
- Remove the complete sample profile section and its unused image/reference.

## Diaspora page
- Add a realistic, full-width photographic banner showing a Nepali returnee entrepreneur or diaspora connection in a recognizably Nepali setting.
- Rewrite the banner and supporting sections around the path from overseas experience to a viable business at home.

## Technical details
- Generate and store two cohesive documentary-style banner images locally, one for Research and one for Diaspora.
- Preserve responsive layouts, dark mode readability, accessibility text, existing interactions, and page-specific SEO metadata.
- Delete the board route cleanly and remove all remaining references without editing generated routing files by hand.
- Verify the site build and inspect the changed pages on desktop and mobile-sized layouts.
