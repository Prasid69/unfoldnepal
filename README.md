# Unfold Nepal Insights

Lovable AI Prompt — UnfoldNepal Website

Build a professional, trust-building website for UnfoldNepal (domain: unfoldnepal.org.np), a non-profit-distributing research and publishing organization based in Nepal.

Organization

UnfoldNepal researches cottage, small, and medium-scale businesses (CSMEs) across Nepal's districts and publishes:

Research reports analyzing business sectors, market opportunities, and success factors.

A flagship book series profiling 100 successful businesses running across different districts of Nepal.

Mission: Help the Nepali diaspora discover real, replicable business ideas rooted in Nepal, encourage them to return and invest, and create local employment — connecting global Nepali talent/capital back to home districts.

Registered activities include: business and employer membership organization activities, market research and public opinion polling, publishing, and related research/advocacy work.

Audience

Nepali diaspora worldwide (US, UK, Gulf, Australia, Japan, Korea, Europe) considering returning to start a business

Researchers, journalists, and policymakers interested in Nepal's SME sector

Potential donors, partners, and institutional funders

Domestic entrepreneurs and students

Goals for the site

Build immediate credibility and trust (this is a serious research/publishing org, not a blog)

Clearly communicate mission and impact

Make reports and the book project easy to discover and eventually purchase/download

Convert visitors into supporters: donors, partners, volunteers, contributors of business stories

Rank well globally in search (diaspora searching "start a business in Nepal," "Nepal SME research," etc.)

Style direction

Modern, minimal NGO/institutional aesthetic — think a hybrid of a think-tank site and a nonprofit annual-report site (e.g., feel of Brookings, Acumen, or Ashoka, but lighter and warmer)

Neutral base palette (off-white/cream + charcoal/graphite text) with one confident accent color (deep amber or terracotta works well to nod to Nepal without being flag-literal) — pick one accent and use it sparingly for CTAs and highlights

Clean sans-serif for UI, a slightly editorial serif for headings/pull quotes to feel "published/research-grade"

Generous whitespace, real photography feel (not generic stock-icon look) — use placeholder images with captions like "Photo: [Business name], [District]"

No logo yet — generate a simple text-based wordmark ("UnfoldNepal") in the header using the accent color, clean and geometric, easy to swap later

Site structure / pages

1. Home

Hero: strong one-line mission statement + subhead + primary CTA ("Read our latest report" / "Support the research")

Impact stats row (districts covered, businesses researched, reports published — use placeholder numbers, clearly editable)

"What we do" — 3 pillars: Research, Publish, Connect Diaspora

Featured report or book preview

Testimonial/quote from a diaspora entrepreneur (placeholder)

Partner/funder logo strip (placeholder)

Newsletter signup + donate/support CTA

2. About

Mission, vision, story/origin

Legal status note (non-profit-distributing organization, Nepal-registered)

Team/advisors section (placeholder cards)

Objectives list (research, publishing, membership/advocacy activities)

3. Research / Reports

Grid/list of research reports by sector and district, filterable

Each report: summary card linking to a detail page (PDF download or "coming soon")

Methodology section explaining how research is conducted

4. The 100 Businesses Book Project

Dedicated landing page — this is the flagship product

Concept explanation, progress tracker (e.g., "32 of 100 profiled"), map of Nepal with district pins

Sample business profile (placeholder case study with photo, founder story, key lessons)

"Nominate a business" or "Submit your story" form

Pre-order / notify-me signup for the book

5. For the Diaspora

A dedicated page speaking directly to Nepalis abroad: "Thinking of starting a business back home?"

How-to-use-our-research guide, FAQs about returning and investing, links to relevant reports

6. Get Involved

Ways to support: Donate, Partner (institutions/CSOs), Volunteer/Researcher, Contribute a story

Contact form + org email/address

7. Blog / Updates (optional but recommended for SEO)

Short articles, district spotlights, diaspora success stories

8. Contact / Footer

Contact form, social links, newsletter, legal/registration info, sitemap links

Functional requirements

Fully responsive, fast-loading

Newsletter signup (email capture) integrated into a form service or waitlist-style component

Contact and "submit a business story" forms with validation

Report/book cards should support future dynamic content (structured so reports can be added easily)

Social sharing meta tags (Open Graph, Twitter Card) on all pages

SEO requirements (important)

Semantic HTML structure (proper H1/H2/H3 hierarchy on every page)

Unique, keyword-rich meta title + description per page (target terms like "Nepal SME research," "small business Nepal," "Nepali diaspora entrepreneurship," "cottage industry Nepal")

Open Graph + Twitter Card tags with placeholder social preview image

Schema.org structured data: Organization markup on homepage, Article/Report markup on report pages

Clean, descriptive URL slugs (e.g., /reports/handicraft-sector-bagmati, not /page?id=12)

Sitemap.xml and robots.txt

Alt text on all images (descriptive, not decorative)

Fast Core Web Vitals — optimized images, minimal render-blocking assets

Internal linking between related reports, districts, and the book project page

Tone of voice

Credible, warm, and hopeful — like a research institution that genuinely believes in Nepal's potential. Avoid generic charity-speak ("help the poor"); this is about opportunity, dignity, and enterprise, not aid.

Please build this as a multi-page site (not single-page scroll), with a clean shared navigation and footer, and use placeholder but realistic sample content (sample report titles, a sample business profile, sample stats) so the site looks complete and populated, not empty.
